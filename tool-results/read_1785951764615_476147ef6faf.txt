     5→     5→     5→// ============================================================
     6→     6→     6→// Design Principles:
     7→     7→     7→//   - Multi-tenant: tenant_id on every business table
     8→     8→     8→//   - Composite unique constraints prevent cross-tenant data leaks
     9→     9→     9→//   - Soft delete (deleted_at) on transactional entities
    10→    10→    10→//   - Audit fields: created_at, updated_at, created_by, updated_by
    11→    11→    11→//   - Double-entry accounting foundation (chart_of_accounts, journal_entries)
    12→    12→    12→//   - Index strategy optimized for reporting queries
    13→    13→    13→//   - snake_case table names via @@map
    14→    14→    14→//   - Singular table names for consistency
    15→    15→    15→// ============================================================
