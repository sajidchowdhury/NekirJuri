    10→## 🎨 Design Identity — "Islamic Modern Premium"
    11→
    12→### Core Principle
    13→> *"Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication"* — Every screen should feel like walking into a well-designed masjid courtyard: **spacious, calm, purposeful, and beautiful.**
    14→
    15→### Islamic Design DNA
    16→- **Geometric harmony** — Subtle geometric patterns as decorative accents (not overwhelming)
    17→- **Calligraphic influence** — Arabic/Bengali typography treated with respect and proper font pairing
    18→- **Arch motif** — Soft arch shapes for cards, modals, and containers (inspired by Islamic architecture)
    19→- **Generous whitespace** — Reflects the spaciousness of Islamic architecture
    20→- **Green as sacred** — Emerald/jade green as the primary brand color (color of Islam)
    21→- **Gold as accent** — Warm gold for highlights, CTAs, and premium features
    22→- **Symmetry & order** — Balanced layouts reflecting geometric precision

   113→## 📐 Spacing & Layout System
   114→
   115→### Spacing Scale (4px base)
   116→`4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 64 · 80 · 96`
   117→
   118→### Container Widths
   119→
   120→| Breakpoint | Sidebar | Content | Max-width |
   121→|------------|---------|---------|-----------|
   122→| Mobile (<640px) | Hidden (sheet overlay) | Full width | 100% |
   123→| Tablet (640-1024px) | Collapsed (icons only, 64px) | Fluid | 100% |
   124→| Desktop (1024-1440px) | Expanded (256px) | Fluid | 100% |
   125→| Wide (>1440px) | Expanded (280px) | Centered | 1440px |
   126→
   127→### Card System
   128→- **Border radius**: `0.75rem` (12px) — soft but not pill-shaped
   129→- **Padding**: `p-6` (24px) for content cards, `p-4` (16px) for compact cards
   130→- **Gap**: `gap-6` between cards in grid
   131→- **Shadow**: `shadow-sm` default, `shadow-md` on hover/elevated
   132→- **Border**: `1px solid var(--border)` — subtle, always present
   133→
   134→### Islamic Arch Border Radius
   135→- For hero cards and stat cards: Use a custom `border-radius` with a subtle arch shape on top — `12px 12px 12px 12px` with an optional decorative top border in `--primary` color (3px)

   139→## 📱 Responsive Strategy — Mobile-First
   140→
   141→### Breakpoint Behavior
   142→
   143→| Breakpoint | Sidebar | Navigation | Tables | Forms |
   144→|------------|---------|------------|--------|-------|
   145→| **Mobile** (<640px) | Sheet overlay | Bottom tab bar | Card list view | Full-width stacked |
   146→| **Tablet** (640-1024px) | Collapsed icon rail | Top breadcrumb | Responsive table | 2-column grid |
   147→| **Desktop** (1024px+) | Full sidebar | Sidebar + breadcrumb | Full data table | 3-column grid |
   148→
   149→### Mobile-Specific Patterns
   150→- **Bottom tab bar** for main navigation (5-7 items max)
   151→- **Swipe gestures** for card actions (edit, delete)
   152→- **Pull-to-refresh** for data lists
   153→- **FAB (Floating Action Button)** for primary create actions
   154→- **Sheet/Drawer** for forms instead of modals
   155→- **Card list** instead of tables for data display
   156→- **Collapsible sections** for long forms
